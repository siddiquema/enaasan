How enaasan's guides are made

Every guide on enaasan is conceptualised by Siddique Ahamed, drafted with the help of AI tools, then reviewed and published by him. AI speeds up the research and writing. The judgment about what goes live is human.

You can read more about how the site is built, and what that means for you, on the About page.

  • ConceptualisedThe questions, the structure and what students actually need to know come from Siddique.
  • DraftedAI tools help with research and a first draft.
  • ReviewedSiddique reads, corrects and shapes what is written before it goes anywhere.
  • PublishedHe takes responsibility for what appears on the site.

What to expect from the guidance

enaasan is a thinking tool. It is not a counsellor and it is not a coaching institute. It informs decisions; it does not make them.

Exam dates, eligibility, fees and cut-offs change. Please verify anything time-sensitive with the official source before you apply or pay.
