# publications/careers/gig-work/

## Purpose

Knowledge Cards and a future Guide on gig and field work — shown as a skill ladder, not as a single "delivery job". Serves the student who is offered (or considering) gig work and needs to judge it honestly, and the parent who sees only its lowest level.
