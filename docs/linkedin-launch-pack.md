## 1. Rules for every post

1. **Every number traces to a report's Sources table.** Section 5 lists each number in the posts and where it comes
   from. Do not add a number that is not in that list.
2. **One idea per post.** One chart or image, three short lines of insight, one link.
3. **Say what a number is.** Reports 1 and 3 are ratios and counts of one source each; Report 2 uses a survey. Never
   claim a cause the data cannot show.
4. **No company, party or minister names.** Opinion goes in a post clearly marked as the author's view.
5. **Replies are drafted by Claude and approved by Siddique before anyone posts them** (section 4).
6. **Post as the Page.** On the Page admin view, use Create, then check the author line says Enaasan before posting.
7. **Links.** Put the link in the post text. Check each link opens before posting.

## 4. Handling comments and messages

Claude drafts, Siddique approves, Siddique posts. Claude does not post or reply.

1. Paste the comment (or a screenshot) to Claude.
2. Claude returns: a suggested reply, and a one-line note if the comment is a question, a correction, a complaint, or
   spam.
3. Siddique edits and posts the reply as the Page.

**Reply principles**

- Thank people, briefly. Answer the question asked.
- If someone corrects a number, check it against the report's Sources table before replying. If they are right,
  say so, and fix the report.
- Do not argue about causes. Say "the data does not show why".
- Do not give personal career, legal, financial or tax advice. Point to the relevant guide or to HR or a
  qualified adviser.
- Do not name companies, parties or ministers, even when asked.
- Send people who share personal details to a private route, and do not repeat those details publicly.
- Spam, unsolicited sales messages, and links with no context are ignored, not opened.
