# Enaasan surveys: plan and final questions

Draft of 2026-11-16. Three anonymous surveys whose results will be published as analysis on
enaasan.online. Follows `docs/research-reports.md` (the hard rule on statistics) and the
design conditions already set in issue #98.

Status: **draft for owner review. No form has been created and no data collected.**

## 1. What we are trying to find out

| Survey | Who answers | Research question |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Students | Currently studying in college (any year) | How do students choose a career direction, and how prepared do they feel for getting a job? |
| 2. New professionals | Working, in their first two years | What did the first months of work demand that college did not prepare them for? |
| 3. Job seekers | Finished their course, looking for work | Where does the job search break down, and what help would they use? |

Read together, the three describe one journey: preparing, searching, starting.

## 2. Rules that apply to all three

1. **Anonymous.** No name, phone, email, roll number, college name or employer name. No optional
   contact field. Microsoft Forms set to "Anyone can respond" with "Record name" off.
2. **Adults only.** The first question screens out anyone under 18. (School students are covered
   separately by #98, which needs school permission.)
3. **Consent on the first screen**, using the text in section 3. No consent, no survey.
4. **Mostly fixed-choice questions**, so answers can be counted. One optional free-text question
   per survey, with an instruction not to write names.
5. **Same background questions in all three** (section 4), so the surveys can be compared.
6. **About three minutes.** 12 questions or fewer after the background block.
7. **English for this round.** Decided by the owner on 2026-11-16. A regional-language round (Tamil, Telugu,
   Hindi) will be run later and analysed and published separately. The English report must say that people
   who are not comfortable answering in English are under-represented in it. The later round should reuse
   these questions unchanged, translated and checked by a reviewer, so the two rounds can be set side by side.
   The two rounds are reported separately and are not added together.
8. **Pilot before launch.** 5 to 10 people per survey. Fix any question they hesitate over. Pilot
   answers are discarded, not counted.

## 3. Consent text (first screen of each form)

> **About this survey**
> This survey is run by enaasan.online, a free career guidance site for Indian students. It takes
> about three minutes.
>
> - It is anonymous. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, college or employer.
>   Please do not write any of these in your answers.
> - Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question or stop at any time.
> - We will publish a summary of the answers, as counts and percentages, on enaasan.online.
>   Individual responses will never be published. Short quotes from written answers may be used,
>   with anything identifying removed.
> - You must be 18 or older to take part.
>
> **Do you agree to take part?** Yes, I am 18 or older and I agree / No (ends the survey)

## 8. Analysis plan (fixed before any data is collected)

**What will be reported**

- For every fixed-choice question: the count and percentage for each option, and the number who answered.
- These breakdowns, where group sizes allow: by course (B4), by first-generation status (B7), by where the respondent grew up (B6), and by gender (B2).
- Across surveys: readiness (S5) set beside preparation (W4); salary expectation (S9) beside the in-hand surprise (W5); help wanted by students (S10) beside help wanted by job seekers (J12).
- Free-text answers: grouped into themes by hand, with the count per theme and a few quotes with anything identifying removed.

**Rules for publishing**

1. **Minimum sample.** Do not publish a survey's results until it has at least 100 completed responses. Do not show any subgroup with fewer than 30 respondents; merge it into "Other" or leave it out.
2. **Say who answered.** Every chart and headline states the number of respondents and says "of those who answered this survey". Never "Indian students" or "graduates in India".
3. **State the limitation plainly.** This round was offered in English only; a regional-language round follows separately. Respondents chose to take part and were reached through enaasan, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and colleges that agreed to share the link. They are not a random sample, and the results cannot be generalised to all students, professionals or job seekers.
4. **No comparisons between colleges or companies.** None are collected.
5. **Publish the method.** The questionnaire, the dates the survey was open, how the link was shared, the number of responses and how incomplete responses were handled all go in the report's Methodology section.
6. **No changing questions mid-run.** If a question must change after launch, the earlier answers to it are reported separately or dropped.

The 100 and 30 thresholds were proposed by the drafting assistant and confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-16. They are an editorial choice, not a statistical standard, and the report's Methodology section should say so.

## 9. Tool and data handling

- **Microsoft Forms**, in the owner's personal Microsoft 365 account. One form per survey.
- Before sharing, open each link in a private browser window and confirm that no owner name or email is shown.
- Check the per-form response limit for the account and close the survey before reaching it.
- Raw responses stay in the owner's account. Only summary counts go into the repository and the report. No raw export is committed to GitHub.
- Microsoft Forms may record a response time and, depending on settings, other metadata. Do not use or publish anything beyond the answers.

## 10. How people reach the surveys

- A "Share your experience" link on First Job Desk, leading to a short page that sends each visitor to the right survey.
- WhatsApp and LinkedIn posts by the owner.
- Placement officers who agree to forward Survey 1 to students and Survey 3 to recent batches.
- No incentives or prize draws in the first round, since those need contact details.
