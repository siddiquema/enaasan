For placement officers and faculty

A free interview practice tool for your students.

First Job Desk works on any phone, needs no sign-up and costs the college nothing. This page has everything you need to share it with a batch.

What students get

Mock interviews with the interviewer's notes

Five interviews: a general HR round, customer support, software trainee, accounts trainee and sales trainee. After each answer the student reads what the interviewer would have written about it, and how to answer better.

Offer Letter Decoder

Students enter the figures from their salary table and see the difference between CTC and monthly in-hand pay. Eight common clauses are explained, each with a question to ask HR.

First 180 Days

Nearly fifty situations from the first six months at work, such as missing a deadline, getting no feedback or a probation review, each with what to do and what to say.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses or answers. Nothing a student types leaves their phone.
  • It does not guarantee placement or predict a real interview result.
  • It does not replace your training sessions. It gives students something to practise with between them.

A 30-minute session you can run

  1. 5 minutes. Share the link. Each student opens the mock interview for the role closest to their target.
  2. 12 minutes. Students complete the six questions on their own phones.
  3. 8 minutes. In pairs, each student reads out one interviewer's note that surprised them and says why.
  4. 5 minutes. As a group, list the three habits that appeared in every strong answer.
