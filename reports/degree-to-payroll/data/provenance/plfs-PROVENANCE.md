Source: MoSPI Publications/Reports page (https://mospi.gov.in/publications-reports), category "Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)". Downloaded 2026-11-04 by repo owner.

| File | Published | Content |
|---|---|---|
| PLFS 2025 Labour Market Snapshot of Selected Districts (Sept 2026).pdf | 18-09-2026 | LFPR, WPR, UR (age 15+) and NEET (age 15-29), Usual Status (ps+ss), rural+urban, for the state and up to 10 most populous districts per state. Based on PLFS 2025 (Jan-Dec 2025). Appendix A (all districts, CWS and ps+ss, RSE, sample sizes) is a hyperlinked Excel file, not in the PDF. |

Not used: PLFS Monthly Bulletin Sept 2026 (national only, no state data); not stored in the repo.

## Caveats
- Usual Status (ps+ss) counts self-employed, unpaid family workers and casual labour as employed, so a low unemployment rate says nothing about formal job availability.
- District estimates rest on small samples. The report marks districts with under 250 surveyed households with "#" and high-RSE estimates (40%+) with "*".
- Estimates use the district frame of Nov 2024.
