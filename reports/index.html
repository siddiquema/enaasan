Research Reports

Quarterly reports on what Indian students study, where work is heading, and the gap between the two — written for students, parents and the people who guide them.

Our standard

A report is only useful if you can check it. Every Enaasan report follows these rules:

  • Every number has a source. Each figure links to a named public dataset and table — such as PLFS, AISHE or AICTE — or to our own survey, whose method we publish.
  • No invented, estimated or modelled statistics. If a number cannot be traced, it is left out.
  • Opinion is labelled as opinion. Practitioner experience is useful, and it is always marked separately from the data.
  • Corrections are public. If we get something wrong, the report shows what changed and when.
